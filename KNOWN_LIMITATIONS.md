# Known limitations

- This is a 3D arcade prototype, not an ultra-realistic simulator.
- Aircraft and scenery are procedural models; detail is limited compared with professionally authored PBR assets.
- Flight physics are simplified and are not aerodynamically validated.
- Hostile AI is basic movement/close-range damage, not advanced tactical AI.
- Radar and target acquisition are simplified UI representations.
- Performance depends on device GPU, browser/WebView, resolution, and thermal limits.
- The APK workflow creates a debug APK for testing. A production release requires a protected signing key and release-signing configuration.
